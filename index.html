Monumental fine art projects supported by international institutions

Sustainability & Fine Art

Life of a Box

Granted official patronage by the Ministry of the Environment of the Czech Republic, Life of a Box transforms discarded industrial packaging donated by global cities and organizations into monumental museum sculptures, raising international awareness for ecological sustainability and resource conservation.

Supported by Nobel Prize Museum, UNESCO Paper Museum Fabriano, Municipal Museums of Nuremberg, and Cotroceni National Museum.
Global Participatory Fine Art

W O R D S …

A global research project inviting world leaders, Nobel laureates, scientists, and cultural icons to donate a single word carrying personal or societal weight. The artist transmutates these diverse linguistic contributions into monumental abstract paintings, creating a unified tapestry of global human thought.

Participating networks include Tate London, Yale University, Svalbard Seed Vault, Sorbonne University Paris, and National Galleries of Scotland.
Indigenous Language & Heritage

Voices of the Land

Dedicated to indigenous First Nations communities, this project honors ancient wisdom and oral traditions. By thoughtfully incorporating living language concepts—such as Ojibwemowin Ankosé ("everything is connected") and Cree sâkihitowin ("love")—into fine art, it creates an enduring bridge between ancestral knowledge and contemporary society.

In collaboration with National Gallery of Canada, Harvard University, University of Alberta, and Burke Museum.
Monumental Mural Architecture

World of Water

Spanning 165 m² across interior walls and ceilings, World of Water is one of Europe's largest hydroelectric mural installations. Located in the Bohemian Paradise region, the monumental artwork depicts Egyptian mythology and celebrates water as a renewable energy source across human history.

Permanent architectural mural installation in Přepeře II Hydroelectric Power Station, Czech Republic.
